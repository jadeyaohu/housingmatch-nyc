## Summary
Baseline QA of the live production site https://housingmatch-nyc.vercel.app, run 2026-11-09 in English and Chinese, at phone (390x844) and desktop (1280x800) sizes. Home page fits everywhere and the EN/中文 switch works with one tap. Matcher, 3 people / $85,000: 63 results shown — 5 Likely match, 58 Needs review, 26 Unlikely hidden (89 total). Matcher, 2 people / $60,000 / Brooklyn / voucher: 38 shown — 2 Likely, 36 Needs review, 51 hidden. Browse filters work but only after pressing Apply, show no result count, and have no clear button; some cards show "—" for rent or borough and two listings appear twice. Lottery "Official listing" links open the correct Housing Connect pages; Pronto re-rental links open a generic NYC search page instead of the building. 12 screenshots attached (home + results + listings, each size x language, exact viewports).

9. Plain-words confusion notes (first-time user, little tech experience). (1) The grey "65,000" sitting in the income box looks like the site already filled in an income — it is only an example and is ignored. (2) On Browse housing you have to press "Apply" or nothing happens; the filters do not work on their own. (3) "Show unlikely matches (26)" is a small tick box; it is not clear that 26 homes are hidden until you tick it, so you may think the 63 shown are all there are. (4) Lots of cards show "—" where the rent or the borough should be, so you cannot tell what a home costs or where it is without clicking away. (5) "No listings to show yet" sounds like the page is still loading, not like "nothing matches your filters." (6) The "Official listing" arrow on re-rental homes (Pronto) does not take you to that home at all — it takes you to a general search page for the whole city, so you have to find the same building again yourself. (7) Two homes appear twice in the list under the same name (5203 Center Blvd, 5241 Center Blvd), which looks like a mistake. (8) "Likely match" and "Needs review" sound similar; the small "Why" button — the only place that explains them — is easy to miss. (9) Ticking "I have a housing voucher" changes nothing you can see, so you cannot tell whether it helped. (10) The Browse page never says how many homes matched, so you have to count the cards yourself. (11) In Chinese, the card tag still says "Re-rental" in English and the voucher box mentions "Section 8, CityFHEPS" in English with no explanation. (12) "Closing soon" includes homes whose deadline is today (2026-11-09), which is easy to miss.
