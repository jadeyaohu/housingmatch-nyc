An unapproved Muse record that takes that path could become publishable. Reproduced with the actual mapping round-trip; fixed by rejecting missing provenance in Production.

3. **P2 — Freshness alone overstates availability.** Unknown/upcoming secondary records, future opening dates and future-dated observations could be labelled verified open. Reproduced and fixed; a missing deadline on a primary lottery still yields unverified, not verified open.
